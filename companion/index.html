Roam Live Companion

Read your Twitch chat, listen to messages and control OBS from your Android phone.

For your stream

Chat, speech and controls.

Use the features you need and keep your quick controls close by.

Twitch chat

Sign in with Twitch to read and send messages in your channel. Open a stream preview when you want to check the picture.

Text-to-speech

Listen to chat using your phone’s installed voices. Choose the voice, speed and volume, and whether to read usernames. Skip a message or clear the speech queue.

Optional OBS controls

Connect to your OBS computer to switch scenes, control its broadcast and check a preview. Your computer runs OBS and sends the broadcast.
