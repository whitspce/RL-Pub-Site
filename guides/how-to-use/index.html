The gear opens Settings. On the main screen, MIC opens microphone selection and gain, while the microphone icon mutes audio. LAYERS controls overlays included in your broadcast. The built-in chat panel is for you to read on the phone.

Before your first stream

Set a destination in Settings > Stream, choose a video quality preset and check your microphone. Return to the camera screen and hold HOLD TO GO LIVE to start. Tap the stop control to end the stream. For destination setup, follow the direct streaming guide or OBS guide.

Main-screen controls

Some controls depend on your phone, settings or connection state. Settings > Customisation changes which toolbar icons appear and their order.

SettingsThe gear opens the Settings categories below. It remains available when other toolbar icons are hidden or reordered.

Tap an uncovered part of the camera preview to focus. Pinch to zoom. The second-camera inset helps you check your framing; choose its corner in Camera settings.

Settings: Stream

  • Stream URL: enter the full destination supplied by your streaming service or receiver. The help button explains the field.
  • Save current URL: name the current destination for reuse. Tap a saved destination to select it, or its remove button to delete it.

Stop streaming before changing the active destination.

Settings: Video quality

Choose a preset before going live. Higher quality needs more upload capacity and can use more power. Auto bitrate reduces the video target when the connection struggles and raises it again as the connection recovers.

While live, Live output shows the current sent bitrate. This includes audio and transport overhead, so it can differ from the selected video target.

Settings: Audio

  • Microphone check: speak and watch the meter. Bluetooth microphone levels appear once you are live.
  • Input gain: use the slider to adjust microphone level. Lower it if loud speech sounds distorted.
  • Microphone: select Default (system choice) or an available device. After plugging in a microphone, leave this category and return to refresh the list. The help button explains microphone choices.
  • Show audio levels HUD icon on home screen: shows or hides the main-screen MIC meter and its quick controls.

Settings: Camera

  • Image stabilization: smooths handheld movement on supported phones and slightly crops the picture.
  • Dual camera: makes the main-screen dual-camera control available. This feature needs a supported phone and can increase heat substantially.
  • Picture-in-picture: choose Small, Medium or Large, then one of four corner positions. Mirror front camera flips the front-camera inset; leave it off when viewers need to read text in that view.

Settings: Overlays

Overlay editor adds text, images and web pages to your broadcast. Main-screen Layers control shows or hides the LAYERS shortcut for changing saved layers' visibility.

Use a web overlay to show viewer chat, alerts or another service's browser overlay. Web pages use additional battery. Their sound can play on your phone, but Roam does not mix it directly into the stream's audio. Your microphone can still pick up sound from the phone speaker.

Overlay editor controls

Open Settings > Overlays > Overlay editor. The preview shows your draft layout. Select a layer in Layers to open its Edit controls. On wider screens, both panels appear together.

Add a layer

  • Text: adds a text layer. Enter your wording, then choose its size, colour and position.
  • Image: opens the phone's image picker. Use an image for a logo, frame or other static graphic.
  • Web: shows a power-use notice, then Add web overlay creates a web layer. Paste a complete HTTPS address into URL, or choose Import HTML or ZIP for a local overlay. Only import files you trust; they can run code and make internet requests.

An imported web layer shows its filename. Use a web address instead returns to the URL field. Web layers fill the broadcast frame; arrange their content through the overlay service or HTML. The editor shows a web placeholder, so check the saved result on the main screen.

Manage layers

Layer nameSelects the layer for editing. The optional Name field helps you recognise it later.

The built-in Roam watermark is protected from deletion. You can still hide it, rename it, reposition it and change its stacking order.

Size, position and live text

  • Text size changes text from 12 to 144. Colour offers eight preset text colours.
  • For images, Size changes width from 5% to 150% of the frame while keeping the image's proportions. Values above 100% let a graphic extend beyond the frame.
  • Position offers nine shortcuts: TL, T and TR for the top; L, C and R for the middle; BL, B and BR for the bottom. X moves horizontally and Y vertically. Text and image layers can be fine-tuned with these sliders; the watermark uses the nine preset positions.
  • Insert live value adds a token at the text cursor: Time, Date, Day, Battery, Bitrate, Stream time or Compass. You can combine tokens with ordinary text. An unavailable value appears as a dash.

Additional location tokens can be typed into Text: {speed} for km/h, {speed_mph}, {altitude}, {lat}, {lng}, {heading} or {city}. These need location permission and use more battery while active. Coordinates and city names reveal your location in the broadcast.

Save or leave

Save applies your draft, including to a running stream, and keeps the editor open. The button briefly reads Saved. The back arrow closes the editor. If changes remain, choose Save to apply them and close, Discard to leave without those changes, or Keep editing to return to the draft.

Settings: Chat

Show chat panel puts messages on your phone for you to read. Choose Text size, Panel width and Panel side, then enter a Kick channel name, Twitch channel name or YouTube channel handle.

These fields select channels; they do not log you into an account. YouTube chat needs an active live broadcast and relies on an unofficial interface, so service changes can affect it. To put chat in the video your viewers receive, add a web overlay instead.

Settings: BRB & Stealth

  • BRB message: choose the text shown during a local break. Choose image from phone replaces that message with your own picture; Clear returns to the message.
  • Recording dot: adds a red reminder that you are live while Stealth is active. This is separate from the REC indicator for saving a local recording.
  • Haptic pulse: makes the phone vibrate while in Stealth. Choose a Pulse interval of 30 or 60 seconds.

Settings: Reliability

  • Record to phone while streaming: saves a copy to Movies/Roam. It uses extra storage and power. The help button explains local recording.
  • Keep trying for: choose how long Roam attempts to reconnect after a dropout: 1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes or Forever.
  • Open battery settings: opens Android's settings for allowing Roam to keep running in the background.

Settings: OBS

These settings connect Roam to OBS's controls. Set Roam's video destination separately under Stream. See the OBS setup guide for the complete connection.

  • Show OBS control on main screen: shows or hides the OBS shortcut. Hiding it does not disconnect OBS or turn off streaming sync.
  • Host (LAN IP or hostname), Port and Password (required): enter your OBS WebSocket details, then use Connect or Disconnect.
  • Sync OBS streaming with Roam: starts an OBS broadcast when Roam starts. When Roam stops, it only stops an OBS broadcast that it started.
  • BRB scene in OBS: chooses the OBS scene controlled by Roam's BRB button. Leave it blank to use Roam's local break screen.
  • Mute mic and camera during OBS BRB: also protects the phone's microphone and cameras during the OBS break. Protection stays active until OBS confirms the break has ended.

Settings: Customisation

Wrap puts toolbar controls on extra rows when needed. Compact puts excess controls under More. Under Main screen icons, show or hide supported icons, move them up or down in the order, and open their related settings. Reset to default restores the toolbar layout.

The Settings gear is always last. Dual-camera availability is controlled in Camera settings.

Settings: Help & about

  • Diagnostics: save or send a log soon after a problem so it includes useful recent information.
  • Export backup: creates a password-protected backup of your settings, overlays and saved connections. Keep the password so you can restore it.
  • Import backup: replaces this phone's Roam setup with a saved backup. Stop streaming before exporting or importing.
  • Check for updates automatically and Check now: check for updates to APK installations. Google Play manages updates for Play installations.
  • Help and feedback, Privacy policy and Third-party licenses: open the relevant information.
